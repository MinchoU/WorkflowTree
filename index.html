Keyboard & Mouse ✕
  • ＋ Add new task — create a card
  • Drag empty space — marquee-select multiple cards
  • Drag a selected card — move the whole selection together
  • Shift + drag — snap to another card's center
  • Drag blue dot → another card — connect with an arrow
  • Drag gray dot (bottom-right) — resize width
  • ⌥ Option + drag — duplicate a card
  • Click a card, then Shift + C + click another — swap their positions
  • Delete — remove the selected card(s) / arrow
  • ⌘Z / Ctrl + Z — undo (last 5 actions)
  • Space + drag or middle-mouse drag — pan  ·  Scroll — zoom
  • ⇄ Sort — auto-layout by arrows & dates
  • Esc — close this dialog
